# GovAsset 360: 3-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00 - 0:30 | The Hook & Dashboard (Admin Role)
*(Start on the Login Page, click Demo Login. The Dashboard loads.)*

**Speaker:** "Government asset management is broken. Roads, buildings, and vehicles are tracked across dozens of disconnected spreadsheets. Today, we are introducing **GovAsset 360**—a unified intelligence platform for government infrastructure."

**Action:** Point out the KPI cards.
**Speaker:** "Right away, as an Administrator, I have a top-down view of our entire infrastructure: 1,400 assets, 38 active projects, and critical maintenance alerts."

## 0:30 - 1:15 | The Asset Lifecycle & Deep Dive
*(Click on 'Assets' in the sidebar. Click into 'National Highway 44 - Sector B')*

**Speaker:** "Let's look at a critical piece of infrastructure. Here is National Highway 44. Instead of just seeing a static record, GovAsset 360 connects everything."

**Action:** Switch to the 'Lifecycle' tab.
**Speaker:** "We can instantly visualize the asset's lifecycle timeline—from Planning to Operational and into Maintenance. We know exactly what phase it is in and who is responsible."

## 1:15 - 2:00 | Issue Tracking & Role-Based Access
*(Click on 'Issues' in the sidebar)*

**Speaker:** "When a discrepancy is found, Field Engineers log it directly from the site. Here we see an open 'Severe Potholes' issue."

**Action:** Go to the top right header, click "Simulate Role" and switch to "Field Engineer". Navigate to Assets.
**Speaker:** "Security is built-in. If I switch my role to a Field Engineer on the ground, you'll notice the 'Register Asset' button disappears. They can only view data and log issues, ensuring strict data governance across departments."

*(Switch role back to Admin. Go to Issues, click Resolve on an issue)*
**Speaker:** "As an Admin, I can review the work and instantly resolve the discrepancy, updating the record across the entire platform in real-time."

## 2:00 - 2:45 | AI Intelligence Assistant
*(Click on 'AI Assistant' in the sidebar)*

**Speaker:** "But the most powerful feature is our Intelligence Engine. Managing thousands of records is impossible for a single officer. We integrated Google's Gemini AI directly into the platform."

**Action:** Click the suggested question: *"Which assets require attention this week?"*
**Speaker:** "Instead of generating generic text, our AI actively reads the live Firestore database. When I ask what needs attention, it summarizes exactly which assets have open critical issues and pending maintenance records, directly grounding its answers in our live government data."

## 2:45 - 3:00 | Closing
**Speaker:** "GovAsset 360 replaces fragmented spreadsheets with a secure, real-time, AI-powered ecosystem. It saves taxpayer money, accelerates maintenance, and brings full transparency to public infrastructure. Thank you."
